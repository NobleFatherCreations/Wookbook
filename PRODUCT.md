# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing: one self-contained offline HTML file generated by `design/build-decoder-v10.py` from JSON data plus `design/decoder-v10/{app.css,app.js,shell.html}`. No framework, no CDN, no external requests (one sanctioned analytics beacon). Deployed as a Netlify CLI deploy of a single file.

## Users
Confirmed: the primary reader is someone hurting and unsure. They usually arrive on a phone, often late, confused or shaken, and do not know the name of what happened to them. Secondary (not confirmed in detail): learners, advocates and support workers who browse for depth.

## Product Purpose
The Pattern Decoder is a free, offline reference to 310 manipulation and control tactics, each written in plain words: what it looks like, how to tell whether it really fits (including what it is not), what to say or do, and how to stay safe. Success means a person leaves able to name what is happening, with less self-doubt, and knowing a safe next step. It is a reading aid, never a diagnosis.

## Positioning
Most lists of manipulation tactics are jargon glossaries or accusation lists. This one is built around honest uncertainty: every entry weighs evidence in both directions, never labels a person, and treats safety as the first fact for the 105 entries where danger can sit alongside the tactic. It can be searched by everyday words, narrowed by two questions, or browsed by plain groups, so it works for readers who do not know any names.

## Operating Context
Used privately on a personal phone; sometimes read in a hurry, sometimes returned to. Nothing typed leaves the device; saved items live in local storage only. Entries may be printed to take to an advocate, counselor or lawyer.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 310 entries; content, safety notes and scripts are clinically sensitive and must not be altered by design work.
- Safety-sensitive entries (105) must never script announcing leaving, ultimatums, or confrontation.
- Entries describe patterns, never people; no verdict labels.
- Fully offline, single file, American English, hash routing; old v9 links and merged-tactic names must keep working.
- Content structure per entry: in short, understand it, is this it, what you can do, go deeper.
- Reader groups (10) and settings (7) live in `content/codex-final/reader-meta.json`.

## Brand Commitments
Part of Noble Father Creations (House menu, NF seal, brand logo). The existing identity is a celestial star chart: constellations drawn as people, brass accent, night-sky and twilight themes, IM Fell English titles. The author asked for a redesign that builds on the "observatory at night" world and wants rich, cinematic motion.

## Evidence on Hand
Cover plate (engraved celestial chart of people-constellations), wordmark SVG, brand logo, emblem set in the v9 page; 109 documented tactic sequences (how tactics combine) usable as real constellation lines; the full entry data in `content/playbook-data.json`. There are no testimonials, outcomes or statistics, and none may be invented.

## Product Principles
1. Orient before you inform: a distressed reader first needs to feel safe and know where to start.
2. Weigh in both directions: every claim is paired with what would point the other way.
3. Safety before cleverness: motion, beauty and delight never delay or hide help.
4. Plain words win; jargon is explained on tap.
5. Private by default: nothing leaves the device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Reader may be in distress, use a screen reader, a low-end phone, large text, or reduced motion. Keyboard-complete, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, reduced-motion respected everywhere, a visible calm/motion control, no autoplay sound, content never hidden behind animation.
