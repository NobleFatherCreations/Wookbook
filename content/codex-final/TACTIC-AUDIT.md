# Pattern Decoder: the 349 tactics, and a uniqueness audit

Audit run 2026-10-19 on the data as it stands (original short definitions and example quotes). Method: exact and fuzzy name check (clean: 349 unique, no near-identical spellings); TF-IDF definition similarity (coarse, short definitions); then a full read of all 349 definitions. Verdicts are a reading of short definitions only, not of the 38-field entries (most of which are shared boilerplate until the rewrite lands). They need the author's decision.
